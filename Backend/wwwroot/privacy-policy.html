Indexsafe Evolution Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PT Indexim Coalindo (“PT Indexim,” “we,” “us”) processes information when authorized personnel use the Indexsafe Evolution mobile application (“App”). The App supports workplace safety, operational reporting, inspection, permit verification, and related workflows.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features that an authorized user uses, we may process:

  • Account and employment information: employee identifier (NIK), name, job title, department, company, authorized role, work contact details, profile image, and login/session information.
  • Operational and safety information: inspection responses, hazard reports, observations, coaching and safety-talk records, action plans, permit and QR verification results, attendance or scan records, and related comments.
  • Files and evidence: photos, videos, documents, PDF records, and other files that a user chooses to attach to an operational or safety record.
  • Location information: precise device location and coordinates only when the user uses a location-dependent feature, such as recording a field report or viewing/using Safe Map.
  • Camera information: camera access when the user scans a QR code or chooses to capture evidence.
  • Device and app information: app version, device/operating-system information, notification token, connection status, and technical logs needed for security, diagnostics, synchronization, and fraud or misuse prevention.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information to authenticate users; apply role-based access; operate safety and operational workflows; verify permits and QR codes; create and manage reports; enable offline work and later synchronization; send relevant operational notifications; maintain audit trails; protect the App and company assets; and improve reliability and security.

3. Permissions

The App requests permissions only when needed for an available feature. Camera permission supports QR scanning and optional evidence capture. Location permission supports field-report coordinates and map functions. Notification permission supports operational updates. File or photo access is used only when a user chooses to select or share an attachment. A user may decline or revoke a permission in Android settings; some related features may then be unavailable.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for third-party advertising. Information may be accessible to authorized PT Indexim personnel, affiliated companies, contractors, or business partners only where this is necessary for their assigned operational, safety, audit, or system-administration responsibilities.

We use service providers that support App functionality, including hosting and notification-delivery services such as Firebase Cloud Messaging. These providers process data only to provide the relevant service. We may also disclose information where necessary for safety investigations, audits, legal obligations, or the protection of people, company property, and systems.

5. Offline Data

To support offline work, the App may keep a limited local copy of synchronized operational records, authorization data, and pending submissions on the device. This data is synchronized with PT Indexim systems when a connection is available. Users should protect their device with an operating-system screen lock and must not share a device or account with unauthorized persons.

6. Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as needed for employment, operational, safety, audit, legal, and records-management requirements. Retention periods may vary by record type. When data is no longer required, it is deleted or archived in accordance with applicable company procedures and law.

For requests to access, correct, delete, or restrict personal information, or to request deletion of an account where applicable, contact your HR representative, HSE representative, or the designated Indexsafe Evolution System Administrator. Requests are handled subject to applicable law and any legitimate operational, safety, audit, or legal retention obligations.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encrypted transport (HTTPS), authenticated access, role-based authorization, and security monitoring. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure; users must keep their credentials confidential and report suspected misuse promptly through the company’s established support channel.

8. Children

The App is intended for authorized workplace users and is not directed to children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App, operations, law, or security practices. The current version will be published at this address and the effective date will be updated.

10. Contact

PT Indexim Coalindo is the organization responsible for this App. For privacy questions or requests, contact your HR representative, HSE representative, or the designated Indexsafe Evolution System Administrator through your company’s official support channel.

Important: This policy applies specifically to Indexsafe Evolution (Android package: id.indexim.mbs).
